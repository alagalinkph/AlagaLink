ALAGALINK VERSION 2 — SCHOOL PROJECT PROTOTYPE

CONTENTS
- index.html: all page sections and content
- styles.css: responsive layout, warm sage/cream palette, large readable typography
- script.js: English/Filipino toggle, topic search and filters, health-topic detail dialogs,
  sample health log, and appointment-request demonstration

PREVIEW LOCALLY
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css, and script.js together in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome.
4. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and externally hosted photos. The site structure and
   functionality still work if those external assets are unavailable.

PUBLISH WITH NETLIFY
1. Sign in or create an account at https://www.netlify.com/
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the extracted AlagaLink_V2 folder (the folder containing index.html) into the drop area.
4. Wait for deployment and copy the public netlify.app URL.
5. To update the website, make changes to the files and upload the updated folder again.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end educational prototype only. The appointment form does not contact a clinic
or book an actual appointment. The health log is temporary in-memory sample data and is not
saved securely. No real clinician consultation, patient records, diagnosis, prescription,
or medication notifications are provided. Do not enter real patient or health information.
For urgent symptoms, seek emergency care; in the Philippines call 911.
Health information is general education and should be checked against current guidance from
qualified healthcare professionals and reputable sources before formal use.
